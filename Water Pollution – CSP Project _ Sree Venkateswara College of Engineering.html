About Our Project

Water pollution happens when harmful substances such as chemicals, waste, plastics and sewage enter rivers, lakes, groundwater and oceans, making water unsafe for people, animals and plants.

🎯

Objective

To study the causes of water pollution in our area and raise awareness about protecting water resources.

🔍

Scope

Identifying pollution sources, observing their effects, and suggesting practical solutions for the community.

🤝

Community Focus

Engaging local people through surveys and awareness messages about safe water habits.

Insights

Key things we learned during the project.

🏭

Industrial Waste

Factories releasing untreated chemicals and heavy metals are a major cause of contamination.

🚰

Sewage & Domestic Waste

Household sewage and detergents harm aquatic life and spread disease.

🌾

Agricultural Runoff

Fertilizers and pesticides wash into water and cause harmful algae growth.

🛍️

Plastic Waste

Plastics block water flow, break into microplastics and damage ecosystems.

🩺

Health Effects

Polluted water can cause diarrhoea, cholera, typhoid and other waterborne illnesses.

✅

Solutions

Waste treatment, less plastic, proper disposal and awareness make a real difference.

Conclusion

Through this CSP project we learned that water pollution is a serious problem affecting health, wildlife and our future. Every person can help by reducing waste, avoiding plastic, disposing of chemicals properly and spreading awareness.

  • Clean water is a shared responsibility.
  • Small daily actions create big change.
  • Awareness is the first step toward a solution.
